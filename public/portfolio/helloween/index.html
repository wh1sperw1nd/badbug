Step 1: Sunlight

A well-known fact that vampires are night creatures. Spending time in the shadows isn't merely a way to disguise themselves, vampires are light sensitive, and drawing even the most powerful of vampires into full sunlight has been known to cause them to burst into flames, and in some cases even implode.

*Remember that vampires are no dummies. Sunlight is often the perfect payoff to an elaborate plan, after all entrances into darkness have been blocked and the cornered creature has nowhere else to hide as the sun rises.
